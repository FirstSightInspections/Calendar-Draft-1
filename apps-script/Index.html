Services

Field inspections & documentation

Whatever the assignment, I inspect the whole property and document everything: roof, exterior and interior, with photos, measurements and a report. I don't approve claims, promise coverage or set settlement amounts.

Every inspection includes

One visit, the whole property

These aren't separate add-ons. On every inspection I check and document all of it, so nothing gets missed and nobody has to send me back.

Roof

  • Covering type, pitch and shingle exposure
  • Layers, drip edge, underlayment and ice-and-water, where observable
  • Valleys, flashing, pipe boots, ridge caps and vents
  • Shingle gauge measurements
  • Every slope photographed: front, right, rear, left

Exterior

  • All four elevations
  • Siding, trim, fascia and soffit
  • Gutters, downspouts, window wraps and screens
  • Soft metals, vents and collateral damage
  • Siding measurements

Interior

  • Room and damage-area overviews
  • Close-up photos of the damage
  • Wall, ceiling and room measurements
  • Door and window openings
  • Room sketches and damage locations

Storm damage

  • Hail and wind
  • Fallen trees and debris
  • Water intrusion
  • Affected and unaffected areas, for comparison

Roof access

  • Ladder setup and repositioning
  • Two- and three-story rooflines, where conditions permit
  • Steep roofs with proper safety equipment
  • Ladder assist for adjusters and engineers on site

What you get

  • Detailed photo documentation
  • Measurements and sketches
  • An organized inspection report
  • Clear notes on what was observed

About

Hi, I'm Ross.

First Sight Inspections, LLC is an independent property inspection and field services company based in Joppa, Maryland, in Harford County.

I'm not a roofing contractor or an adjusting company. My job is to inspect, document, measure, photograph and report property conditions accurately, thoroughly and on time, so the people I work with can make informed decisions.

It's a mobile business: I travel to every property, often several in a day across different counties and states. That's why this booking page shows where I'm already working, so you can pick a day that fits the route.
